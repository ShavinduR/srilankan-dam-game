Privacy Policy

App: Dam — Sri Lankan Draughts · Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Dam game (the website and the Android app) uses, and why. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Information you provide

  • Display name. When you tap “Find Match” you type a display name. It is sent to our game server and shown to your opponent during the match. It is kept only in the server's temporary memory while you are connected and is never saved to a database. It is erased when you leave or the server restarts.
  • Random session ID. Each browser tab creates a random ID (not linked to your name, device or account) so that if your connection drops, the server can return you to your game. It is kept only in server memory for the duration of the game.
  • Private room codes. If you create a room, a short code (e.g. DAM-4821) is generated. Anyone with the code can join that one game. Codes expire after 30 minutes or when the game starts.
  • Game moves. Your moves, game actions (resign, draw offers, nudges) and the board state are sent to the server so both players see the same game. They are not stored after the match ends.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your last display name, preferred board size, computer difficulty, sound setting, “Blow hints” setting and your game statistics (games played, wins, losses, draws, streaks) in your device's local storage. Games against the computer run entirely on your device. This data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

3. Technical information

To deliver the game over the internet, our hosting providers — Vercel (website) and Render (game server) — process your IP address and basic connection details (such as browser/device type) and may keep them in short-term server logs for security and reliability. We do not use this information to identify you.

4. What we do NOT do

  • No user accounts, passwords, email addresses or phone numbers are collected.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Please don't use your real full name as a display name.

6. Data deletion

Because nothing is stored on our servers after a match, there is no account or saved data to delete. Clearing the app's storage removes everything kept on your device.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
